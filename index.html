Why finance, honestly

[ the short version ]

I did not grow up around it. I got here sideways. Running SEYA meant reading a P&L every week whether I wanted to or not, and I started noticing the questions I cared about were valuation questions. What is this worth, to whom, and why. Which of my decisions actually moved that number.

The search fund seat was the first time I saw people ask those questions for a living. Since then every room has added a layer: how a small check gets to yes, what makes a business sellable versus just good, how a founder's QuickBooks becomes an adjusted EBITDA bridge somebody will pay a multiple on.

What I want next is reps. Real models, real diligence, real feedback from people who have done it longer than I have. I like the private side of investing because the work is closer to the business itself: you can see the inventory, call the customers, and be wrong in a way you can learn from.

The operator half of me is not going anywhere. I think it is the useful half.
