SEYA is the clothing brand I co-founded before college. We sold mostly through college students posting about us, on 10+ campuses in the US and Canada, and did six figures in sales in our first year.

The SEYA story

Why finance?

Running SEYA meant looking at our numbers every week: margins, cash, what to reorder and when. I started caring more about those questions than about the clothes. What is a business worth, and which decisions actually change that?

So I started interning at places that answer that for a living: a search fund, then a VC firm, then two investment banks. I still really like it. Right now I want to get very good at the modeling and diligence work. Long term, I'm most interested in private investing, where you get close to the actual business.
